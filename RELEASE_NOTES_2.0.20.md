# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.20

## Faction Specialist Workstations

- Vampire MCA villagers can become Vampire Experts at an Altar of Inspiration.
- Hunter MCA villagers can become Hunter Experts at a Hunter Research Table.
- Werewolf MCA villagers can become Werewolf Experts at a Stone Altar.
- The original faction totems still work as job sites, and the Priest still uses the Altar of Cleansing.

## Easy Villagers Trader

Put the appropriate villager in a Trader, then right-click the Trader with its faction's progression block. The normal Easy Villagers workstation flow assigns the specialist job while retaining the MCA visual snapshot, faction state and offers.

If the stored MCA villager belongs to another faction, the Trader refuses the block without consuming it. Normal villagers and all other workstations retain Easy Villagers' original behavior.
