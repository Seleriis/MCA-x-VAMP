# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.39

## Village Favors Through Conversation

Village favors now belong in the MCA conversation where they feel natural.

- Eligible residents in active special villages can offer **Could you use a hand?** in their normal conversation menu.
- The resident explains the request and the player chooses **I'll help.** or **Not now.** there, without chat buttons or commands.
- The deadline starts only after accepting. Speak with the same resident again to see the remaining request or give up the favor.
- Accepted residents remain outlined only for the player helping them.
- Infected human residents of Hunter villages can ask directly for milk treatment.

Existing accepted missions keep their progress, target, deadline and outline. Existing unanswered requests become untimed offers until the player accepts them through conversation.

All gameplay rewards, faction restrictions, supply quantities, cooldowns and villager data formats are unchanged.
