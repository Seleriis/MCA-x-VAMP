# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.1.7

## Reliable special Vampirism offers

MCA Vampires now receive the native Vampirism special-offer roll at the exact
point MCA generates their offers. This covers their initial professional list,
Cycle Trades and normal profession refreshes.

The chance and contents remain unchanged: each generated list has Vampirism's
one-in-three chance to add one heart, emerald or blood-bottle opportunity.
