# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat

MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat lets MCA villagers become Vampires, Hunters and Werewolves while remaining real MCA villagers. Their identity, appearance, family, social data and relationships are preserved.

Highlights:

- faction-aware combat, trades, social interactions, curing and village capture;
- vampire night schedules, sunlight behavior and Hunter pitchfork handling;
- Easy Villagers support through mca-ev-compat, including captured faction state, Trader rules and Cycle Trades safety;
- alternate faction-specialist workstations;
- the 30-page Book of Occult Arts guide;
- configurable MCA village bounds for vampire fog and compat-owned special-village effects;
- optional OP level-2 diagnostics with `/mca_vamp_compat`.
- faction gift preferences and recovery diets that still respect MCA's normal gift saturation.
- native Werewolves transformations are safely blocked for MCA villagers, keeping their MCA identity intact.

Requires Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.20 and Java 17. The verified MCA Reborn / Vampirism combinations are 7.6.16+1.20.1 with 1.10.15 and 7.6.28-beta.10+1.20.1 with 1.10.16. Werewolves 1.20.1-2.0.2.7 and Easy Villagers 1.20.1-1.1.23 through 1.1.39 are optional. The captured-villager, Trader and Cycle Trades bridge also supports optional mca-ev-compat 1.0.2; absent optional mods safely disable their integrations. Inventory Viewer integration is not part of this 1.20.1 release.
