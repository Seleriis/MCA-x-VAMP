# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.27

## Fixes

- Administrative `inspect` and `resync` commands now find loaded MCA villagers by their visible name.
- Names with spaces can be written in quotation marks.
- UUIDs and selectors such as `@e[type=mca:male_villager,limit=1]` continue to work.

## Compatibility

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.234, MCA 7.7.25+1.21.1 and Vampirism 1.10.12.
