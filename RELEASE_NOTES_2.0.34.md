# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.34

## Village Favor Polish

- Supply requests now receive a fair quantity when they are created: 2–4 full Blood Bottles, 4–10 Garlic, or 4–10 Raw Mutton. Vampire Fangs and Garlic Injections remain single-item requests.
- Mission quantities are shown in bold white text and remain fixed until the request is resolved or cancelled.
- The Crimson Reserve accepts full Blood Bottles from anywhere in the inventory, so three separate bottles can be delivered together without changing Vampirism's non-stackable bottle behavior.
- Mission reminders now state the exact item and quantity still needed. Urgent Hunter Aid reminders clearly ask for milk.

## Compatibility

- Existing active missions remain valid and keep their previous fixed quantities when upgrading.
- No villager NBT, traits, faction state, or configuration keys were changed.
