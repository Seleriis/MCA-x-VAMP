# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.23

## MCA Villager Editor faction traits

Saving a change to an MCA villager's traits now updates the full faction immediately.

- Adding **Vampire**, **Hunter** or **Werewolf** applies that faction's gameplay state, appearance and equipment.
- Removing one of those traits removes the matching faction state cleanly, including vampire appearance and attributes, werewolf features, Hunter alignment and the temporary pitchfork.
- The editor resolves accidental conflicting faction traits to one stable faction, so a villager cannot retain mixed faction data.

Existing village control, professions, trade rules, curing and Easy Villagers storage are unchanged.
