# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.17

## Vampire night schedule

- Adult MCA Vampires now follow MCA's native nocturnal schedule: they rest in their bed during the day and resume professional work at night.
- Player orders and combat continue to take priority. If no bed is available, the villager keeps MCA's safe rest routine and the existing sunlight shelter fallback.
- The prior MCA schedule is preserved and restored after a cure, role change, capture/placement or world reload.

### Configuration

- `vampireVillagersUseNightSchedule` is enabled by default. Set it to `false` to keep the normal MCA timetable.

No faction, trade, capture, inheritance or book-delivery rules were changed.
