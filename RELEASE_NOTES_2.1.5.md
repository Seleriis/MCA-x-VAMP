# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.1.5

## Vampirism special trades for MCA Vampires

MCA villagers that become Vampires can now receive the same special trade
opportunity as Vampirism's native converted villagers. When their professional
offers are generated, the mod uses Vampirism's native one-in-three roll to add
one of its converted-villager offers, including heart, emerald and blood-bottle
trades.

- The MCA villager remains an MCA villager: name, appearance, relationships,
  profession, offers and all MCA data are retained.
- The existing faction-price overlay uses the real offer list, so uses, XP,
  demand, Shift-click transactions and Cycle Trades remain native and safe.
- Cycle Trades generates a fresh special-offer opportunity before the first
  completed trade, including in the Easy Villagers Trader.
- The special offer is removed if the Vampire is fully cured or its Vampire
  state is removed with the MCA editor.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.234, Java 21, MCA
7.7.36-beta.3+1.21.1 and Vampirism 1.10.12.
