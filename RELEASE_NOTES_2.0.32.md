# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.32

## Occult Village Favors

- Eligible MCA residents in active Vampire, Hunter and Werewolf villages can ask nearby players for supplies or help.
- Give the full requested stack through MCA's normal **Gift** action. A completed favor consumes only the requested amount and grants five relationship hearts with the resident who made the request.
- Hunter villages may ask for urgent milk treatment of a specific infected human MCA villager. The favor completes only after the normal milk treatment has actually cleared the infection.
- Each player receives one request per special-village totem during the configurable cooldown, set to one Minecraft day by default.
- Adds four Arts Occultes advancements, optional Werewolf requests, and page 31 of the Book of Occult Arts.

## Safety

- Mission progress is stored independently from villagers. No faction state, trait, UUID, capture data, trade data or AI is changed.
- Players recognized as enemies do not receive or complete village favors. Gifts unrelated to an active request retain MCA's normal behavior.
