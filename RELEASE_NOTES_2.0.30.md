# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.30

## Arts Occultes

- Adds the **Arts Occultes** advancement tab to Minecraft's normal progress screen, led by the existing **Secrets Beyond the Veil** discovery.
- Adds advancement goals for vampire, hunter and werewolf villager interactions, early treatment and complete cures, inherited babies, Easy Villagers pickup and faction specialist workstations.
- Werewolves and Easy Villagers leaves appear only when their optional integrations are installed.

## Faction Refusals

- Refused faction trade, capture and specialist-workstation actions now show a small matching icon before the existing message: red eye, golden garlic or silver wolf paw.
- No faction rules, prices, player data or villager payloads changed.
