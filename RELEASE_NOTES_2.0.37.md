# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.37

## Village Mission Dialogue Polish

Village favors now sound like requests from the residents themselves instead of system prompts.

- Every invitation, accepted-request reminder, cancellation and expiry response has been rewritten in Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese and English.
- Vampire, Hunter and Werewolf residents each speak in their own voice.
- Remaining time is part of the resident's message, rather than a separate technical deadline line.
- The Village Favors page in the Book of Occult Arts and the related advancement descriptions now use the same player-facing language.

This version does not change mission rewards, supply amounts, deadlines, cooldowns, faction permissions or saved mission data. Older active missions remain valid; because they predate deadlines, they do not receive a fabricated timed reminder.
