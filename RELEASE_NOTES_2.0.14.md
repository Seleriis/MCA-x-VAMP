# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.14

## Cycle Trades capture hotfix

- Fixes Cycle Trades being unavailable for an MCA villager with a profession after it is captured and placed again through Easy Villagers, even when the villager has never completed a trade.
- The button now relies only on a completed-trade marker and actual used offers. Profession XP no longer counts as a completed trade.
- Villagers that have completed a trade remain permanently unable to reroll their offers, including after capture, placement, storage, world reloads and restarts.

No faction, price, offer, Trader, capture or book behavior was changed in this hotfix.
