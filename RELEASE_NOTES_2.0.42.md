# MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat 2.0.42

## Faction Gifts and Recovery Diets

Faction villagers now judge gifts by their nature without bypassing MCA's own gift-saturation system. A favorite remains valuable, but repeating it too often still loses value and may be refused just as it does for ordinary MCA gifts. Enemy factions remain unable to offer normal presents, including through the MCA dialogue command.

Vampires value blood, fangs and other vampire materials but reject ordinary food, garlic and milk that cannot cure an active infection. Werewolves favor meat, pelts, teeth and liver while refusing vegetarian foods, bread, sweets and vegetable stews. Hunters favor garlic, filled injections, weapons, shields and armor.

Recovery now follows each nature: Vampires drink 100 mB from a blood bottle over their normal recovery use, and Werewolves recover only with the Werewolves raw or cooked food tags. Humans and Hunters keep MCA's normal recovery diet.

Garlic injections are still valid cure ingredients. Every successfully accepted injection costs the applier 10 MCA relationship hearts with that villager, once and only once for that ingredient.
